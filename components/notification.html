Notification

Notifications inform users about important events, confirmations, errors, or warnings. They come in message (toast) and alert (inline) variants.

Message – Info

Title only

Your session is about to expire.

Title + body

New update available

Version 3.4.1 includes performance improvements and bug fixes. Refresh to apply.

With CTA button

Your export is ready

Your CSV file has been generated and is ready to download. This link will expire in 24 hours.

Alert – Danger

Your account is suspended

Access has been restricted due to a billing issue. Please update your payment method to restore access.

Failed to save changes. Please try again.

Alert – Positive

Payment successful

Your subscription has been activated. You now have access to all Pro features.

Profile updated successfully.

Alert – Warning

Your trial ends in 3 days

Upgrade to a paid plan to keep access to all your projects and data after the trial period ends.

Storage is almost full. You are at 90% capacity.
